Compliance Migration Story

10 Years of Gmail Archives
Moving to Outlook

When RegTech Solutions landed a major government contract, compliance requirements forced them to abandon Gmail. But they couldn't lose a decade of searchable email history.

Rachel Thompson, Chief Compliance Officer at RegTech Solutions, read the contract clause three times. They'd just won their biggest contract ever—$18M over five years with a major government agency. But there was a requirement she hadn't anticipated.

Section 12.4 - Email Systems Compliance:

"Contractor shall maintain all email communications on Microsoft 365 Government Cloud (GCC High) infrastructure. Third-party email platforms (including but not limited to Google Workspace) are prohibited for handling Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI)."

RegTech had been on Google Workspace since day one. Ten years. 320 employees. Millions of emails. Their entire business ran on Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar.

The contract was too valuable to pass up. But migrating from Gmail to Outlook wasn't just about switching platforms—they had critical requirements that made it exponentially harder.

The Non-Negotiable Requirements

Complete History Preservation

Every email from 2015-2025 must be searchable in Outlook. No data loss, no gaps in the archive.

Folder Structure Intact

All Gmail labels must convert to Outlook folders without losing organization.

Metadata Preservation

Original timestamps, sender/recipient info, and headers must remain accurate for audits.

90-Day Deadline

Migration must complete before contract start date—3 months away.

The Gmail Export Problem

Google Workspace exports email in MBOX format—a single massive file per user. Outlook can't directly import MBOX files. And their attempts to use Google's built-in migration tools hit walls:

Google's Migration Tool
Only migrates recent emails, not full 10-year archives. Historical data would be lost.

The Three-Phase Migration Strategy

1

Export Phase

Use Google Takeout to export all 320 accounts to MBOX format. Create an intermediate EML extraction for maximum compatibility.

2

Conversion Phase

Batch convert MBOX/EML archives to PST format with preserved folder structures and metadata for Outlook compatibility.

3

Import & Validation Phase

Import PST archives into Office 365 mailboxes, validate data integrity, and verify searchability for compliance.
